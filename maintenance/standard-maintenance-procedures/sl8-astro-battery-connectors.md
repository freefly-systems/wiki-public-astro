# SL8/Astro Battery Connectors

**We recommend all Astro pilots routinely check the aircraft and SL8 battery connectors for signs of damage, debris, or excessive wear.**

## Inspect Connectors&#x20;

* Inspect the battery connectors on Astro for melted plastic and / or solder at the base of the power pins (they are located in the center).&#x20;
  * **Do not fly Astro if the connector shows evidence of electrical damage** and contact Freefly Support for further guidance .&#x20;
* Inspect all SL8 battery connectors for melted plastic, debris, or dirt.&#x20;
  * **Do not fly any SL8 battery if the connector shows evidence of electrical damage** and contact Freefly Support for further guidance.&#x20;
* Debris and dirt can be gently removed with compressed air from the SL8 connector
* Astro aircraft side connectors can be cleaned with compressesd air or with a toothbrush and 99% Isopropyl Alcohol&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
Some wear is normal and expected, particularly where the Astro's ground pins make contact with the batteries.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
Never remove debris from the battery with tweezers. If debris cannot be removed from the battery with compressed air, the battery may need to be returned to Freefly or disposed of.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
